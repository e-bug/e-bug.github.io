MSc student at EPFL

I’m a Master’s student in the school of Computer and Communication Sciences at EPFL, where I’m a member of the Data Science Lab led by Prof. Robert West. My interests lie in data science, machine learning and deep learning to gain insights from large amounts of data. I’m currently working on my Master’s thesis at Technicolor Research in Palo Alto, CA.

Previously, I was a Research Assistant at the Swiss National Supercomputing Centre working on distributed TensorFlow and a Teaching Assistant for Internet Analytics, taught by Prof. Matthias Grossglauser.
